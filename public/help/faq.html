# Frequently Asked Questions ## General ### Q1: What is Agorich Pharma? Agorich Pharma is a pharmaceutical distribution management system with AI features for inventory, sales, and payment tracking. ### Q2: Who can use it? Roles: Super Admin, Admin, Distributor, Retailer, Sales Team ### Q3: Do I need internet? Yes, it's cloud-based. Mobile app works offline but syncs when online. --- ## AI & Voice Calls ### Q4: What is Voice AI? An automated calling system that calls customers about overdue payments in Hinglish. ### Q5: How do I know if AI made a mistake? Check the customer's profile and invoices. Report errors to support. ### Q6: Can I override AI decisions? Yes! Use the Approval Queue to approve/reject AI actions. ### Q7: Can I turn off Voice AI? Yes, go to Admin → Settings → Voice AI → Toggle OFF --- ## Command Center ### Q8: What is Command Center? An AI-powered query tool. Ask questions in plain language. ### Q9: How do I access it? Press `Ctrl + K` or click the chat widget on dashboard. ### Q10: What can I ask? Sales, inventory, payments, reports - in simple language. --- ## Approvals ### Q11: What needs approval? Discounts >15%, write-offs >₹5000, price changes >10% ### Q12: How do I approve? Go to Notifications → Approval Queue → Approve/Reject --- ## Data & Security ### Q13: Is my data safe? Yes! Encrypted, access controlled, audit logged. ### Q14: Who can see my data? Only your organization. Others cannot access. --- ## Costs ### Q15: How much does AI cost? Depends on usage. Check Admin → Spending Dashboard ### Q16: Can I set limits? Yes, admins can set monthly budgets. --- ## Still Have Questions? **Contact:** tech@agorichpharma.com **More:** [Full FAQ](../docs/faq.md)
