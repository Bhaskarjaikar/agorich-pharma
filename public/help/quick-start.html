# Quick Start Guide **⏱️ Time to Complete: 5 minutes** Complete these steps to get started with Agorich Pharma! --- ## ✅ First Steps Checklist - [ ] 1. Log in to your account - [ ] 2. Explore the Dashboard - [ ] 3. Try the Command Center - [ ] 4. Check your first alert - [ ] 5. Review pending approvals --- ## Step 1: Log In Go to: https://app.agorichpharma.com Enter your email and password. Contact tech@agorichpharma.com if locked out. --- ## Step 2: Explore the Dashboard When you log in, you'll see the main Dashboard: | Section | What It Shows | |---------|---------------| | Overview | Today's sales, AR, tasks | | Alerts | Items needing attention | | Quick Stats | Key metrics | | Command Center | AI assistant chat | Take 2 minutes to explore each section! --- ## Step 3: Try Command Center (Most Important!) **How to open:** Press `Ctrl + K` or click the chat box **Try these queries:** 1. "Hello" - AI greets you 2. "Show my sales today" 3. "Who owes me money?" 4. "What is low in stock?" 5. "Any expiring items?" --- ## Step 4: Check Alerts Go to **Notifications** → **Alerts** - 🔴 Low stock alerts - 🟡 Expiry warnings - ✅ System notifications --- ## Step 5: Review Approvals Go to **Notifications** → **Approval Queue** - Review pending requests - Approve or reject - Add notes --- ## 🆘 Need Help? - **?** icon - Context help - **Email:** tech@agorichpharma.com - **Hours:** Mon-Sat, 9 AM - 6 PM --- **Next:** See the full [User Guide](user-guide.html)
